Project 30: Audit Evidence System

Focus: Evidence Management & Audit

Overview

Centralized evidence repository with 8 evidence types (logs, tickets, certificates, etc.), 12-month validity tracking, finding status management, gap analysis, and JSON export for audit reports.

Key Features

  • 8 evidence types (logs, tickets, screenshots, documents, etc.)
  • 12-month evidence validity tracking
  • Finding status management (open, remediated, accepted)
  • Gap analysis and compliance gaps
  • JSON export for audit reports
  • Evidence chain of custody
  • Audit trail and version control
  • Finding severity and tracking

Hiring Relevance

  • For Equity Bank: Audit evidence collection and management
  • For internal audits: Enterprise evidence repository
  • Shows audit and compliance documentation expertise
